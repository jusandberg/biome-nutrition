The human side of change

Knowing what to do is only part of the picture.

What we eat is influenced by more than nutrition knowledge. Appetite, energy and nutritional needs matter. So do habits, motivation, preferences, environment and experience.

You may already know a lot about nutrition. You may have tried different approaches or collected more advice than you know what to do with. The challenge is figuring out what actually applies to you—and how to make it workable.

We can pay attention to

  • the routines and cues that shape how you eat
  • the difference between intention and what happens on a busy day
  • motivation that changes over time
  • preferences, access, schedule and environment
  • all-or-nothing patterns that make consistency harder
  • small adjustments you can practise and refine

This is not psychotherapy, and I do not represent myself as a psychologist. Behaviour-change support here stays focused on nutrition, eating patterns, habits, motivation and practical barriers within nutritional counselling.
