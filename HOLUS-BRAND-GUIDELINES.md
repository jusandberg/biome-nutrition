# Hōlus Nutrition Counselling brand guidelines

Status: approved working system, version 1  
Last consolidated: September 26, 2026

The interactive companion is [`brand-guide.html`](brand-guide.html). It contains live previews, downloadable logo and favicon SVGs, colour-copy controls, typography samples, icon downloads, and illustration examples.

## Brand foundation

- Visual wordmark: **hōlus.**
- Formal business name: **Hōlus Nutrition Counselling**
- Descriptor: **NUTRITION COUNSELLING**
- Tagline: **Nutrition that begins with you.**
- Brand-name casing: lowercase for the visual identity; capitalized in formal documents
- Character: modern, intelligent, warm, human, evidence-informed, refined, and established
- Avoid: tech-startup, SaaS, pharmaceutical, clinical, overly geometric, playful, childish, futuristic, or generic-wellness treatments

## Wordmark

The approved visual wordmark is **hōlus.** in lowercase, including the macron over the `o` and the final period. Use **Hōlus Nutrition Counselling** in policies, emails, invoices and professional documents. Use **HOLUS** only where plain uppercase letters are required, such as trademark searches.

- Typeface: Manrope Light (`300`)
- Letter spacing: approximately `-0.045em`
- Primary letter colour: Ink `#243341`
- Wordmark colour: Ink `#243341`
- Descriptor: Manrope Bold, uppercase, widely tracked, in Sage Deep `#4B5D49`
- Minimum screen width: 90 px; use the favicon below this size
- Clear space: at least the wordmark's x-height on every side
- Approved backgrounds: White, Mist, Ink, and Sage Deep

On Ink or Sage Deep, use white lettering. Keep the descriptor quiet and secondary.

Do not bold, stretch, skew, recolour, outline, or add effects to the wordmark. Do not remove the macron or the final period. Do not replace it with a generic symbol, leaf, molecule, DNA motif, or other logo device.

## Favicon

The approved favicon is **a horizontal macron above a centred dot**.

- Primary tile: Ink `#243341`
- Macron: Sage Light `#C7D2C4`
- Dot: White `#FFFFFF`
- Shape: circular tile
- Concept: the macron from the wordmark, with the individual at the centre
- It must remain legible at 16, 32, 96, and 192 px

For dark-interface use, the alternate may use Sage Light as the tile with Ink for both marks.

Approximate use across a composition:

- White: 60%
- Ink: 20%
- Mist: 12%
- Sage family: 8%

Use colour with restraint. Ink carries authority; sage provides emphasis; white and pale neutrals create calm and space. Do not introduce unapproved decorative colours.

## Typography

### Manrope

Use Manrope for the wordmark, headings, labels, buttons, and navigation.

- Wordmark: `300`
- H1: `800`, tight spacing around `-0.04em`
- H2–H3: `800`, tight spacing around `-0.03em`
- Eyebrows and labels: `800`, uppercase, approximately `0.14em` tracking
- Buttons and navigation: `700`

### Inter

Use Inter for body copy and longer reading passages.

- Body: `400`
- Supporting emphasis: `500–600`
- Default body line height: approximately `1.6–1.7`

Typography should feel editorial and assured. Preserve strong headline scale, generous whitespace, concise copy, and comfortable reading measure.

## Iconography

Use custom duotone line icons.

- Base size in the guide: 32 px viewBox
- Stroke: Ink, `1.6px`, rounded caps and joins
- Secondary fill: Sage Light, offset slightly from the line drawing
- On dark backgrounds: white stroke with Sage Deep fill
- Keep the drawings simple, calm, and human rather than app-like

Approved subject families include nutrition, life stage, advice, routines, digestion, movement, sleep, goals, water, fibre, plants, and conversation.

Avoid emoji, filled app-style glyphs, icons inside decorative circles, literal food imagery everywhere, and stock wellness symbols.

## Illustration

The illustration language uses **orbits, leaves, connected nodes, and soft ground shapes** in the approved palette.

Use illustration for:

- Section art and page headers
- Digest covers and social posts
- Places where photography would otherwise be used

Avoid:

- Scales, tape measures, and before/after imagery
- Busy scenes, clip art, or stock-photo aesthetics
- Colours outside the approved palette
- Medical, pharmaceutical, or generic wellness visuals

Illustrations should reinforce connection, context, and the individual at the centre. They should remain secondary to the message.

## UI and layout expression

- Use white or warm neutral grounds with thin neutral borders
- Use rounded corners consistently with the website
- Preserve generous but purposeful whitespace
- Keep headings in black or deep ink and body copy in muted grey
- Use sage for restrained emphasis, not decoration
- Keep motion subtle; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Use visible focus states with Sage and sufficient offset
- Keep the visual identity editorial, not like a dashboard or wellness app

## Voice

Write in clear, warm, practical language. The voice should be evidence-informed without sounding clinical, and personal without sounding promotional.

- Focus on real life, context, choice, and practical next steps
- Avoid perfection language, moralizing food, jargon, and sales-funnel phrasing
- Prefer direct, human terms such as “introductory chat”
- Keep the visual wordmark lowercase with its final period: **hōlus.**

## Governance

These guidelines are the source of truth for future Hōlus Nutrition Counselling design work. The central site brand values remain in [`brand-config.js`](brand-config.js). Update the written guide, interactive guide, configuration, favicon, and production implementation together when an approved brand decision changes.

The interactive guide may demonstrate future or newly approved treatments before they are applied to the production site. A production change should be made only when it is explicitly requested and visually checked at desktop and mobile sizes.
