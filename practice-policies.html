Practice information

Clear expectations, from the beginning.

These policies explain how somaē handles personal information, appointments and the scope of nutritional counselling.

Privacy Policy

Information you share when contacting the practice or receiving services is used to respond to you, provide nutritional counselling, maintain appropriate records and meet professional or legal obligations. It is not sold or used for unrelated marketing.

Email is convenient but may not be fully secure. Please avoid sending sensitive health information by email unless we have agreed on an appropriate way to share it. Questions about privacy can be sent to hello@theumethod.ca.

Terms

Website information is educational and general in nature. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or emergency care, and it does not create a practitioner-client relationship until services have been formally arranged.

If you have urgent symptoms or a medical emergency, contact an appropriate regulated healthcare professional or emergency service.

Cancellation Policy

Please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged in full, except where an emergency or exceptional circumstance makes notice impossible.

Scope of Practice

Nutritional counselling includes nutrition education and practical food and lifestyle strategies. It does not include diagnosing or treating disease, interpreting medical tests, or prescribing or adjusting medication.

When a concern requires medical assessment or care outside this scope, referral to a physician or another appropriately qualified professional will be recommended. Nutritional counselling supports medical care and does not replace it.

Contact

For appointments, policy questions or general inquiries, email hello@theumethod.ca.
